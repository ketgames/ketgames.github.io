Idle Craft 3D

*Idle Craft 3D* is an engaging casual game where your journey begins after a meteorite shower unexpectedly destroys your home. Now, the mission of rebuilding begins, and it’s up to you to manage the workforce! Hire dedicated workers to gather resources and reconstruct each part of your home, but keep an eye on their stamina—they’ll need rest after a hard day’s work. When workers doze off, summon wise elders to motivate them back to action. This game offers a charming blend of resource management and strategic planning, inviting you to appreciate the hard work and resilience of tireless laborers. Dive into *Idle Craft 3D* to explore new areas, unlock exciting content, and discover endless rebuilding fun!
